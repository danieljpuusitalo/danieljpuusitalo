Daniel Uusitalo

Daniel Uusitalo is a venture capitalist at 4impact capital, where he invests in early-stage European software companies across the Nordics, DACH and Benelux. Born in Finland and based in The Hague, he is a Climate Reality Leader and writes about start-ups, artificial intelligence and climate tech. This is the full archive of his published writing, plus selected interviews and commentary published about him. For headshots and bio copy, see the press kit; for photographs, the photo archive.
