FILE 01 / BIO

BORN IN FINLAND.
INVESTED IN EUROPE.

Daniel Uusitalo is a venture capitalist at 4impact capital, where he invests in early-stage European software companies across the Nordics, DACH and Benelux. Born in Finland and based in The Hague, he is a Climate Reality Leader and writes about start-ups, artificial intelligence and climate tech.

Pre-seed to Series A, with a focus on energy and climate tech. The working thesis: the next generation of European category winners will be impact companies by default, not by label. Finding them early is the job. Writing about it keeps me honest.

I spend a lot of time in the Nordics, where I think founders have historically been too humble about what they're building. I write and speak about that, along with energy tech, European competitiveness, and what it takes to raise capital as an early-stage founder.

Off the clock: public equities, Brazilian jiu-jitsu, reading, ice bathing, and building side projects.

01 / CONVICTION

I believe the best companies of the next decade will be built by founders who treat impact as an engineering constraint, not a marketing label. Europe has the talent, the regulation, and the market pull. What it needs is less hesitation and more conviction. I want to find those founders early, back them properly, and write about the patterns I see along the way.
